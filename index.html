The idea behind NextRead

About NextRead

Choosing a book can be difficult when there are thousands of options. NextRead makes the process easier by recommending books based on the reader’s personal preferences.

Readers can select genres, moods, book lengths, age groups, and book types. NextRead then searches its collection and presents books that match those choices.

How Recommendations Work

NextRead compares your answers with information assigned to each book. Books that match more of your selections appear higher in your recommendation results.

Please note: Recommendations are suggestions based on the information available in our book collection. Readers should review the description before choosing a book.
